# 🐘 Ganpati Bappa Morya 🌺

## 🎮 Ganesh Chaturthi — Festival Maker

A divine and colorful festival adventure game inspired by the joy and traditions of **Ganesh Chaturthi**.

The player joins Lord Ganesha on a festive journey through **5 exciting stages**.

## 🌺 5 Stages

### 🪔 Stage 1 — Decorate the Pandal
Run through the festival area and collect flowers, diyas and decorations to prepare the beautiful Ganpati pandal.

### 🎨 Stage 2 — Create Rangoli
Create a beautiful and colorful rangoli using traditional festival patterns.

### 🍬 Stage 3 — Prepare Modaks
Collect ingredients and prepare delicious modaks for Ganpati Bappa.

### 🥁 Stage 4 — Festival Rhythm
Follow the beats of the dhol and complete the festival rhythm challenge.

### 🙏 Final Stage — Divine Celebration
Celebrate the successful Ganesh Chaturthi festival with Ganpati Bappa!

## ✨ Features

- 🐘 Divine Ganesha theme
- 🌺 Ganesh Chaturthi festival atmosphere
- 🪔 Colorful pandal decorations
- 🎮 Interactive gameplay
- 📱 Mobile-friendly controls
- 💻 Laptop keyboard controls
- 🎵 Festival music
- 🏆 5-stage adventure

## 🎯 Goal

Complete all five stages and reach the **Divine Celebration**.
